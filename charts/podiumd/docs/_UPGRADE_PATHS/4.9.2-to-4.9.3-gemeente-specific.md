## Open Beheer HTTPRoute targets the backend (asse, rott, dimp)

In asse test/accp, rott test/accp and dimp accp the HTTPRoute
`hr-openbeheer-nginx` routes to `openbeheer` (the Django backend) instead of
`openbeheer-nginx`, so `/` shows only "This is the Open Beheer backend";
`/admin` and login still work. Fix in the environment
([DS-6649](https://dimpact.atlassian.net/browse/DS-6649)): set `backendRefs`
to `openbeheer-nginx` port 80. See
[IN-2956](https://dimpact.atlassian.net/browse/IN-2956) and
[`openbeheer.md`](../apps/openbeheer/openbeheer.md).
