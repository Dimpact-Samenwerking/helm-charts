# Gemeente-specific notes — PodiumD 4.8.1 → 4.8.2

Findings for this hop that apply to a **specific gemeente or environment** —
not to the release in general — are collected here: data quirks, local
overrides, hosting particulars, incident follow-ups.

## Rotterdam (accp, test)

- `rott/accp/podiumd.yml` and `rott/test/podiumd.yml` pin `nginx-unprivileged`'s
  `tag: "1.29.8"` explicitly (on both the ZAC nginx sidecar and apiproxy) — older
  than even the pre-4.8.2 chart default of `1.31.1`. This predates the 4.8.2
  `1.31.1 → 1.31.3` bump and is unrelated to it: the explicit tag override wins
  regardless of what the chart default is, so these two environments are
  unaffected by the bump either way and stay on `1.29.8` until their own
  `podiumd.yml` is updated.
