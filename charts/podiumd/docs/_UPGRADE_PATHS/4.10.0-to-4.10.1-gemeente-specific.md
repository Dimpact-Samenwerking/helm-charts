# Gemeente-specific notes — PodiumD 4.10.0 → 4.10.1

Findings for this hop that apply to a **specific gemeente or environment** —
not to the release in general — are collected here: data quirks, local
overrides, hosting particulars, incident follow-ups.

## zwol (prod)

- The Objecten `tokenauth` has two items with `identifier: openarchiefbeheer`.
  The IN-2784 script adds the permission to both and reports a CHECK note:
  keep one.

## asse (accp)

- The `openarchiefbeheer` item already has `read_and_write` on
  `REP_ZAC_PRODUCTAANVRAAG_UUID_REP`; the IN-2784 script leaves it unchanged.

## dim1 (ontw), ExternalsPodiumD

- `kiss.settings.logboek.token` is `REP_OBJECTEN_CREDENTIALS_CONTACT_TOKEN_REP`,
  while the `contact` `tokenauth` item has a literal token. The kiss-2 script
  cannot match them and reports a CHECK note: add `read_only` on
  `REP_ITA_ACTIVITEITENLOG_UUID_REP` to the item by hand.

## info (ontw), ExternalsPodiumD

- `kiss.settings.logboek.token` is `REP_CONTACT_OBJECTEN_TOKEN_REP`, while the
  `contact` `tokenauth` item has `REP_OBJECTEN_CREDENTIALS_CONTACT_TOKEN_REP`.
  The kiss-2 script reports a CHECK note. When both placeholders hold the same
  secret, add `read_only` on `REP_ITA_ACTIVITEITENLOG_UUID_REP` to the
  `contact` item by hand.
